# Risk Management & Trade Planning

A central objective of BOZ is bridging the gap between raw data analysis and actionable, disciplined trade planning. BOZ never outputs a prediction without an accompanying risk budget.

## ⚖️ Key Risk Metrics

### 1. Conviction Score (0 to 100)
- Calculated dynamically based on the degree of confluence across Technicals, Macro, News, and Sentiment.
- **80-100**: High confluence across all indicators and timeframes.
- **60-79**: Moderate confluence; caution on position sizing.
- **< 60**: Low confluence / Choppy market; cash or sidelined stance recommended.

### 2. ATR-Adjusted Stop Loss
- Stop loss levels are placed outside standard market noise (typically 1.5x to 2.0x ATR) rather than arbitrary percentages, reducing premature stop-outs during high-volatility regimes.

### 3. Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR)
- Minimum target threshold is 1:2.0 (risking 1 unit to gain at least 2 units).
- Plans with unfavorable RRR (< 1.5) are automatically flagged as substandard or high-risk.

### 4. Explicit Invalidation Criteria
- Clearly states the exact technical condition that renders the trade thesis null and void (e.g. *"Daily candle close below 50-day EMA"*).
